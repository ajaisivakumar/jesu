    01 — About

    About COMFESTA

    COMFESTA 2K26 is the flagship technical symposium of the Department of Computer Science Engineering, built for students who think in code and build in the open.

    Across a single day, the department turns into a sandbox for innovation — where research meets rapid prototyping, algorithms meet artificial intelligence, and quiet coders get a stage. From high-stakes coding contests to AI-assisted builds and campus-wide puzzle hunts, COMFESTA is where technology meets talent, and collaboration outpaces competition.

    05 — Guidelines

    Rules & Guidelines

    Please read carefully before you register

    👥

    Eligibility & Teams

    • Open to UG and PG students from all recognized engineering institutions.
    • College ID card is mandatory at the venue.
    • Team members may belong to the same college or different colleges.
    📋

    Event Conduct

    • Punctuality: Follow event timings strictly; latecomers may forfeit their slot.
    • Discipline: Maintain professional conduct and respect the judges' decisions.
    • Recognition: Certificates are issued to all registered participants.

    🏆 Symposium Judging Criteria

    Weighted

    Innovation & Creativity

    Originality of the idea and uniqueness of the technical approach.

    Weighted

    Technical Depth

    Weighted

    Presentation

    Communication skills, clarity, and time management.
